Privacy Policy

Midnight Quiz. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Discord, X or an email link, we receive your account identifier and, depending on the method, your email address. You also choose a display name. When you finish a quiz, we store your score and which topic it was for.

How we use it

We use this information only to sign you in, to show your display name and total score on the public leaderboard, and to make sure each account plays each topic once. Your email address is not shown to other players.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our database and sign-in provider, Supabase. Your browser also keeps a small amount of data locally, for example to remember that you are signed in.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
